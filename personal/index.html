About

Haibo Wang works on radiation detection and imaging technologies, readout electronics and the instrumentation systems that integrate them. Applications include positron emission tomography (PET), single-photon emission computed tomography (SPECT) and positron annihilation spectroscopy.

Research

Figures are schematic and not to scale.

Detection and imaging

Scintillator and silicon photomultiplier (SiPM) detectors with time-of-flight and depth-of-interaction capability, and their integration into imaging systems.

Applications: PET, SPECT

Readout electronics

Front-end and digital readout for timing, energy and position measurement, including time-over-threshold methods, timing correction and FPGA-based data acquisition.

Applications: PET and SPECT detectors, lifetime spectrometers

Positron annihilation spectroscopy

SiPM-based detectors, coincidence systems and digital spectrometers for positron annihilation lifetime measurement in materials studies.

Applications: materials characterisation, thin films
