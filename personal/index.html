About

I work on detection and imaging technologies, readout electronics and related systems.

My research spans detector design, electronics development, system integration and measurement methods. Applications I have worked on include positron emission tomography (PET), single-photon emission computed tomography (SPECT) and positron annihilation spectroscopy. I am also interested in extending these technologies and methods to other applications.

I received my PhD in Physics from the University of Science and Technology of China in 2022 and subsequently carried out postdoctoral research at the University of California, Davis.

02

Research

My work connects detector characterization, readout development and system-level measurements across three related areas.

Detection and imaging systems

Scintillator and silicon photomultiplier (SiPM) detectors, including time-of-flight and depth-of-interaction measurements. Previous work includes PET and SPECT detector development and imaging system integration.

Readout electronics and instrumentation

Front-end electronics, SiPM array readout and data acquisition for timing, energy and position measurements. This work includes readout circuits, ASIC-based detector integration and FPGA-based coincidence selection.

Positron annihilation spectroscopy

Digital positron annihilation lifetime spectrometers, fast timing measurements and signal processing, with applications in materials characterization.
